🌡️ Temperature

✅ Both France and Canada Use Celsius!

"Il fait 25 degrés" = It's 25°C (77°F). "Il fait moins 20" = It's -20°C (-4°F).
Quick reference: 0°C = freezing, 20°C = comfortable, 30°C = hot, 40°C = heat wave.
"Il fait combien ?" = What's the temperature?

💡 "Au" for Spring, "En" for the Rest

Spring is the odd one out: au printemps. The other three seasons use en: en été, en automne, en hiver. Just memorize that spring is special!

🇫🇷 France Climate

France has diverse climates: oceanic (Brittany, Normandy — mild, rainy), continental (Alsace, interior — cold winters, warm summers), Mediterranean (Provence, Côte d'Azur — hot dry summers, mild winters), and mountain (Alps, Pyrénées — snow-heavy). Paris: mild but gray in winter, warm summers (heatwaves increasingly common: la canicule).

🇨🇦 Canada Climate

Québec winters are legendary: "Il fait moins 30" is not unusual in January/February. Key winter vocabulary: la poudrerie (blowing snow), le verglas (freezing rain/ice storm — the 1998 ice storm is unforgettable), le banc de neige (snowbank), la souffleuse (snowblower). Spring: la fonte des neiges (snowmelt). Summers can be hot and humid, especially in Montréal.

⚠️ "Tuque" — A Canadian Word

Une tuque is the Québécois/Canadian word for a knit winter hat (beanie). In France, it's un bonnet. If you say tuque in Paris, they might not know what you mean!

🗣️ Talking About Weather

Aujourd'hui il fait beau, il y a du soleil et il fait 22 degrés.
(Today the weather is nice, it's sunny and it's 22 degrees.)

Demain il va pleuvoir et il fera plus froid.
(Tomorrow it's going to rain and it will be colder.)

L'hiver dernier, il a fait moins 35 à Montréal !
(Last winter, it was -35 in Montréal!)

📚 Key Takeaways

📌 Lesson Summary

✦ Weather uses three patterns: "Il fait" + adj, "Il y a" + noun, and verbs (il pleut, il neige).

✦ Both countries use Celsius. "Moins 30" (-30°C) is a reality of Canadian winters.

✦ "Au printemps" but "en été / en automne / en hiver."

✦ Key Canadian winter vocab: poudrerie, verglas, tuque, souffleuse, banc de neige.

✦ Tuque (🇨🇦) = bonnet (🇫🇷) = winter hat. Canicule = heat wave.
