A complete CEFR A1–A2 French course — 🇫🇷 France and 🇨🇦 Canada side by side

🌟 What This Course Covers

Twenty-eight lessons take you from your first bonjour to the end of A2: you will be able to handle everyday situations, describe your life, ask and answer questions, and talk about the past and the future. Topic lessons give you the words; the Grammar Essentials lessons woven between them give you the machinery to build your own sentences instead of reciting phrases.

Every lesson compares 🇫🇷 France and 🇨🇦 Canadian usage, because the French you meet in Montréal is not the French you meet in Marseille — and both are worth having.

Built in: audio on every word · a spaced-repetition review deck · flashcards · self-check quizzes · written exercises · a learning journal · progress tracking.
