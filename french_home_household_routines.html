⚠️ The Ground Floor Is Not the First Floor

In France, le rez-de-chaussée is street level and le premier étage is one flight up — so a French "first floor" is an American second floor. In a French lift, the button you usually want is RC or 0, not 1.

💡 Ask for les toilettes, Not la salle de bains

In most French homes the toilet is genuinely a separate little room from the bathroom. Asking Où est la salle de bains ? may get you shown to a room with a bathtub and no toilet. The polite public phrasing is Où sont les toilettes, s'il vous plaît ?

⚠️ faire les courses ≠ faire du shopping

Faire les courses is the weekly food shop. Faire les magasins or faire du shopping is browsing for clothes. Mixing them up produces a mild but very recognisable comedy: Je fais les courses while holding a designer bag.

🇫🇷🇨🇦 Across the Atlantic

🇫🇷 France

Flats are advertised by total rooms, not bedrooms: a T2 or F2 means living room plus one bedroom. Rent is monthly, charges comprises or not, and a deposit is une caution. Moving day is un déménagement, and almost everyone rents unfurnished — often without a fitted kitchen.

🇨🇦 Canada

Québec counts rooms the same way but says it aloud: un trois et demi (3½) means one bedroom, living room, kitchen and the "half" bathroom. Leases famously run July 1 to June 30 — la fête du déménagement. Landlord is le proprio, and heating (le chauffage) is a real line item in the budget.

✍️ Practice

Exercise 1 — Name the room

  1. On prépare les repas dans _____ cuisine. (the) (Answer: la)
  2. On dort dans la _____. (Answer: chambre)
  3. On regarde la télé dans le _____. (Answer: salon)
  4. En France, le _____-de-chaussée est au niveau de la rue. (Answer: rez)

Exercise 2 — Chores with faire

  1. Je _____ la vaisselle après le dîner. (faire — je form) (Answer: fais)
  2. Nous _____ le ménage le samedi. (faire — nous form) (Answer: faisons)
  3. Il faut _____ les poubelles ce soir. (to take out) (Answer: sortir)
  4. Tu peux _____ l'aspirateur ? (to vacuum: passer) (Answer: passer)

Exercise 3 — Your daily routine

  1. Je _____ réveille à sept heures. (reflexive pronoun) (Answer: me)
  2. Elle _____ habille rapidement. (reflexive pronoun before a vowel) (Answer: s')
  3. D'_____, je me couche vers onze heures. (usually) (Answer: habitude)
  4. Je rentre _____ dix-huit heures. (around) (Answer: vers)
