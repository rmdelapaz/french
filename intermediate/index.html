🌟 What This Tier Covers

Twenty-six lessons take you from the end of A2 to a confident B2. The beginner course gave you the machinery to build a sentence; this one gives you the subjunctive, the conditional, the compound tenses, the relative pronouns and the agreement rules that let you build a paragraph — and then an argument.

The second half turns outward. Register, slang, the literary passé simple, idioms, formal writing, authentic media, a full Québécois deep dive, and a DELF strategy lesson to finish. By the end you can read a French novel, follow a news bulletin, write a formal complaint and defend an opinion.

Built in: audio on every word · a separate spaced-repetition review deck · flashcards · self-check quizzes · written exercises · a learning journal · progress tracking.

Every lesson keeps the 🇫🇷/🇨🇦 comparison — and at B2 it finally pays off in register, administrative language and media, not just vocabulary.
