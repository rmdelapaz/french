🇫🇷 Lesson 2: Le Futur Antérieur & Future Time Clauses

You already have two ways to talk about the future: aller + infinitive for what is coming up, and the futur simple for what will be. This lesson adds the last piece — the tense for something that will already be finished by a future moment — and fixes the single most common mistake English speakers make with French time clauses.

🔧 Building the Futur Antérieur

Same recipe as ever: an auxiliary plus a past participle. This time the auxiliary sits in the futur simple.

Auxiliary choice and past-participle agreement are the passé composé rules, unchanged. Three compound tenses now, one set of rules — this is the payoff for the work you did at A2.

📅 Use 1: Finished by a Future Point

The core meaning is "will have done": an action complete before some other future moment.

D'ici demain, j'aurai fini le rapport. — By tomorrow, I'll have finished the report.
Dans deux ans, ils auront déménagé. — In two years, they'll have moved.

⚠️ Use 2: Two Futures in a Row — the Big Rule

Here is where French and English part company. After quand, dès que, aussitôt que, une fois que, lorsque and tant que, English switches to the present tense. French does not: it keeps the future.

EnglishFrench
When I finish, I'll call you.Quand j'aurai fini, je t'appellerai.
As soon as he arrives, we'll eat.Dès qu'il arrivera, nous mangerons.
Once you have read it, tell me.Une fois que tu l'auras lu, dis-le-moi.

💡 How to choose between the two futures

Ask whether the two events are sequential or simultaneous.
One finishes, then the other starts → futur antérieur in the quand clause: Quand j'aurai mangé, je sortirai.
They happen together → plain futur simple: Quand je serai à Paris, je te téléphonerai.

This rule catches almost every learner at least once, because the English sentence gives no signal at all. "Call me when you get home" becomes Appelle-moi quand tu seras rentré — future, not present.

🤔 Use 3: A Guess About the Present

Just as the futur simple can express probability (il sera malade — he's probably ill), the futur antérieur guesses about something already done. This is extremely common in speech.

Careful with un délai: it is a faux ami. It means the time allowed, not a hold-up. A hold-up is un retard. We come back to this family of traps in Lesson 19.

🇫🇷 France

In the workplace you will meet the futur antérieur constantly in project talk: Une fois que le devis aura été validé, nous lancerons les travaux. Note that in casual speech the futur proche often replaces the futur simple in the main clause — Dès que j'aurai fini, je vais t'appeler — but the quand clause keeps its future all the same.

🇨🇦 Canada

Same grammar, but Québécois speech leans even harder on the futur proche in the main clause: Quand j'aurai fini, j'vas t'appeler (with the characteristic j'vas for je vais). You will also hear tantôt doing the work of d'ici peu — and remember it can point backwards or forwards: Je l'ai vu tantôt (earlier) or Je te vois tantôt (later).

✍️ Practice

Exercise 1 — Form the futur antérieur

Put the auxiliary in the futur simple.

  1. Demain, j'_____ terminé le projet. (avoir) (Answer: aurai)
  2. Elle _____ rentrée avant minuit. (être) (Answer: sera)
  3. Nous _____ tout préparé d'ici là. (avoir) (Answer: aurons)
  4. Ils _____ partis quand tu arriveras. (être) (Answer: seront)
  5. Tu _____ compris en deux minutes. (avoir) (Answer: auras)
  6. Vous vous _____ levés très tôt. (être) (Answer: serez)

Exercise 2 — Never the present after quand

Put the verb in brackets into the futur simple.

  • Quand tu _____ à la maison, appelle-moi. (être) (Answer: seras)
  • Dès qu'il _____, nous commencerons. (arriver) (Answer: arrivera)
  • Tant qu'il _____, je resterai à l'intérieur. (pleuvoir) (Answer: pleuvra)
  • Lorsque vous _____ le temps, passez me voir. (avoir) (Answer: aurez)

Exercise 3 — Sequential or simultaneous?

Write futur antérieur or futur simple for the missing verb.

  1. Quand j'aurai fini de manger, je sortirai. The first verb is in the _____ (Answer: futur antérieur|futur anterieur)
  2. Quand je serai à Rome, je visiterai le Colisée. The first verb is in the _____ (Answer: futur simple)
  3. Une fois que tu auras lu le livre, prête-le-moi. The first verb is in the _____ (Answer: futur antérieur|futur anterieur)

Exercise 4 — Supposition

Rewrite the guess using the futur antérieur. Fill in the auxiliary.

  • He must have forgotten. → Il _____ oublié. (Answer: aura)
  • They must have missed the bus. → Ils _____ raté le bus. (Answer: auront)
  • She must have left already. → Elle _____ déjà partie. (Answer: sera)
