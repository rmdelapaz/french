🇫🇷 Lesson 23: Object Pronouns & Linking Ideas

♻️ Why Pronouns Make You Sound Fluent

Beginners repeat nouns: Tu as le livre ? Oui, j'ai le livre. Je donne le livre à Marie. Fluent speakers replace them: Tu l'as ? Oui, je l'ai. Je le lui donne. Same information, half the words, and a completely different impression.

French object pronouns sit before the verb — the opposite of English, which puts them after. That single structural difference is what makes them feel hard, and practising them out loud is what makes them automatic.

1️⃣ Direct Object Pronouns — le, la, les

A direct object receives the action with no preposition: Je vois le film → Je le vois.

2️⃣ Indirect Object Pronouns — lui, leur

An indirect object is reached through à, and is almost always a person: Je parle à Marie → Je lui parle.

FrenchPronunciationMeaning

💡 lui Covers Both Genders

Je lui parle = I'm talking to him or to her. Unlike le / la, the indirect pronoun makes no gender distinction — and leur here never takes an -s (that's the possessive leurs, a different word).

Verbs that take à + person, so use lui / leur: parler à, donner à, dire à, téléphoner à, écrire à, répondre à, demander à, offrir à, montrer à.

⚠️ The Two Languages Disagree About Which Verbs Take What

Téléphoner is indirect in French ("to phone to someone"): Je lui téléphone.
Écouter, regarder, attendre and chercher are direct in French but take a preposition in English: Je l'écoute (I listen to him), Je l'attends (I wait for her).

4️⃣ En — "Of It", "Some of It", "From There"

En replaces de + something: a partitive, a quantity, or a place you're coming from.

FrenchPronunciationMeaning

🚨 The Number Stays When You Use en

English drops the noun and keeps nothing: "I have two." French keeps en AND the number: J'en ai deux. Saying J'ai deux alone is ungrammatical. Same with quantities: J'en veux beaucoup.

When Two Pronouns Meet

The order is fixed. Learn the ladder:

me / te / nous / vous → le / la / les → lui / leur → y → en

Je le lui donne. (I give it to him.) · Elle me les a montrés. (She showed them to me.) · Il y en a. (There are some.)

⚠️ Agreement With a Preceding Direct Object

In the passé composé with avoir, the participle agrees with a direct object that comes before the verb:
J'ai vu la voiture → Je l'ai vue. · Les photos ? Je les ai prises.
It is usually silent, but it is visible in writing and audible with participles like pris / prise.

🔗 Linking Your Ideas

A2 is where sentences start joining up. Two tools do most of the work: relative pronouns and connectors.

qui, que, où

WordRoleExample
quisubject of the next verb (who / which)L'homme qui parle est mon père.

✅ A Test That Never Fails

Look at what comes straight after the gap. If it's a verb, use qui. If it's a subject (je, tu, Marie...), use que. Le livre qui est sur la table (verb follows) · Le livre que j'ai acheté (subject follows).

🇫🇷🇨🇦 Across the Atlantic

🇫🇷 France

Casual speech compresses pronouns hard: Je le lui ai dit becomes "j'lui ai dit"; Il y en a becomes "y'en a". Du coup has largely replaced donc as the filler-connector of choice among younger speakers.

🇨🇦 Canada

Québec favours ça fait que (often "faque") where France says donc or alors: Faque j'y ai dit... Note j'y standing in for je lui — very common in speech, though it stays out of writing.

Exercise 1 — Replace the noun with a pronoun

  1. Je vois le film. → Je _____ vois. (Answer: le)
  2. Tu connais Marie ? → Tu _____ connais ? (Answer: la)
  3. Je parle à mon frère. → Je _____ parle. (Answer: lui)
  4. Elle écrit à ses parents. → Elle _____ écrit. (Answer: leur)
  5. Nous allons à Paris. → Nous _____ allons. (Answer: y)
  6. Je veux du café. → J'_____ veux. (Answer: en)

Exercise 2 — Put the pronoun in the right place

  1. Je ne _____ vois pas. (him) (Answer: le)
  2. Je _____ ai vu hier. (her — before a vowel) (Answer: l')
  3. Je vais _____ téléphoner ce soir. (to him / to her) (Answer: lui)
  4. Regarde-_____ ! (positive command: it, masculine) (Answer: le)
  5. J'_____ ai trois. (I have three of them) (Answer: en)

Exercise 3 — qui, que or où?

  1. L'homme _____ parle est mon voisin. (Answer: qui)
  2. Le film _____ j'ai vu était très bon. (Answer: que)
  3. La ville _____ j'habite est petite. (Answer: où|ou)
  4. C'est un livre _____ raconte la guerre. (Answer: qui)
  5. Je n'aime pas le café, _____ je préfère le thé. (so / therefore) (Answer: donc)
