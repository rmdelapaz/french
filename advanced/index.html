🌟 What This Tier Covers

Twenty-four lessons take you from the end of B2 to genuine C2 mastery. The grammar is essentially finished, and that changes what a lesson is for. The thirteen C1 lessons work on the things a fluent speaker still gets wrong: the subjunctive as a choice, the long sentence, nominal style, the full connective inventory, pronouns and prepositions at the limit, the hard agreements — and then the two systems no textbook teaches, liaison and prosody and the grammar of spoken French.

The eleven C2 lessons are register work on a finished grammar: word history, expert orthography and typography, the French academic genres, legal and business French, rhetoric and langue de bois, literary prose, versification, translation, the politics of the language — and the DALF.

This tier also opens the course out. Québec had its own deep dive at B2; here you meet Belgium and Switzerland, then Africa, the Maghreb and the creole world, where most French speakers now live.

Built in: audio on every word · a third separate spaced-repetition deck · flashcards · self-check quizzes · 96 written exercise groups · a learning journal · progress tracking.
